ABOUT

Bio

I lived most of my life 20 miles outside Chicago. As a teenager I found my love of computers and problem solving, teaching myself how to use a command terminal for no reason other than curiosity. After roughly 20 years living in the same area, I packed my bags and traveled around, living in Texas, Seattle, and even Africa before finding my new home in Indianapolis.

I graduated from Eleven Fifty Academy in 2020 and have spent the past six years working as a software developer. Most of my work has been in C# and .NET, building and maintaining websites, mobile apps, APIs, and the databases behind them.

I've worked with Blazor, MAUI, Xamarin, and ASP.NET MVC, integrated systems like Stripe and Shopify, maintained Azure DevOps pipelines, and released apps through the Apple, Android, and Amazon app stores. Outside of work, I've been building a wish-list app, a game, and tools for managing notes.
